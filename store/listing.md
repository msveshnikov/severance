## en-US

**Title (30):** Severance: Lumon Office

**Short description (80):** Refine scary numbers, smuggle contraband and dance to Defiant Jazz on the severed floor.

**Full description:**

Welcome to Lumon. Your outie has volunteered you for important work.

Severance: Lumon Office is an isometric adventure on the severed floor. Play four innies of Macrodata Refinement — Mark, Helly, Dylan and Irving — and switch between them at any moment. Eighteen directives take you through the white hallways, into Optics & Design, the Wellness Center, the Perpetual Wing… and down the red-marked elevator.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
🔢 Refine macrodata: find the scary numbers by how they move — Woe sinks, Frolic bounces, Dread trembles, Malice twitches — and sort them into the right bins
🫥 Sneak contraband past Mr. Milchick and Mr. Graner's vision cones
☕ Recite the Statement of Compunction, sit through a wellness session, print a keycard at the right temperature
🐐 Feed the baby goats (they aren't ready yet)
🔴 Trigger the Overtime Contingency: two switches, far apart, held at the same moment by two innies
🎺 Celebrate 100% quota with a Music Dance Experience — a rhythm game to a swing loop, the whole department dancing
🧇 …and the waffle party

FEATURES
• Hand-crafted 3D office in a clean isometric view, walls that melt away around you
• Four playable characters, a whole cast of staff, a baby goat
• English and Russian
• No ads, no purchases, works offline

A non-commercial fan game inspired by the series Severance. Not affiliated with Apple TV+ or the show's creators.
